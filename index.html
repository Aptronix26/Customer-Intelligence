What the longer history changes

2025 prior-history recognition rises to 63,463 codes. A four-year lookback catches relationships that a 2024-only match would miss.
3,856 codes persist across all four years. This is the highest-confidence long-term relationship cohort for lifetime-value and ecosystem journey work.
Cross-year name consistency remains extremely high. Exact-code matches are therefore a strong continuity anchor while contact fields are pending.

What we can activate now

Multi-year matched customers: prioritize lifetime value, category journey, repeat frequency and upgrade-cycle analysis.
Prior-history unmatched codes: maintain a reactivation candidate pool, but activate only after contact identity is connected.
2025 first-seen codes: build the relationship using purchase recency and ecosystem gaps without overstating acquisition.
