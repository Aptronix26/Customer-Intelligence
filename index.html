Filtered preview

Downloads contain every filtered row, not only the preview. Revenue, units and lines are additive. Customer and invoice interactions are distinct within each original published cell and become non-additive when summary layouts combine cells.
Public privacy rule: the downloadable file contains no customer names, codes, phone numbers, emails or sales-executive identifiers. Daily Store × City × Channel × Product Family cells with fewer than 10 identifiable customers are not published at granular level; their activity is rolled up to a safe city or national aggregate where possible, and any remaining low-volume rollup is excluded. The status line reports retained net-value coverage. For customer-level marketing activation, use a separate private access-controlled tool.
