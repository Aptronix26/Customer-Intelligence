Executive read: Exact Customer Code continuity across 2024→2025 is 34,235 customers. That is a meaningful confirmed repeat base, but the much larger unmatched populations should not be called “new” or “churned” until a stable identity field lets us identify customers whose Customer Code may have changed. Use the matched population for lifetime-value and ecosystem analysis; treat the 244,924 2024-only codes as a candidate pool pending identity validation.

First seen in available history

Customer Codes visible in 2025 without an exact-code match in 2024.
281,522 Customer Codes are first seen in the available 2024–2025 code history during 2025.
This is not the same as “new customer.” Until mobile/email is available, a changed Customer Code could make an existing person appear new.
Best use: treat as first-seen relationships for current-period engagement while keeping identity confidence explicit.

What we can activate now

Matched 2024→2025 customers: build lifetime value, category journey and repeat-frequency views.
2024-only codes: prepare a dormant/reactivation candidate pool, but activate only after contact identity is connected.
2025 first-seen codes: use purchase recency and ecosystem gaps to build the relationship without calling them definitively new.
